# Third-party references

VOLYA v0.8 is an original implementation. Its architecture and system design were informed by these public open-source projects:

- SerhiiChoGames/tower-defense — MIT License. Reference for Phaser scene/entity separation, tower placement, and model-oriented organization.
- boxops/tower_defense_js — Apache License 2.0. Reference for between-wave countdown, early-send behavior, spawn scheduling, path-progress movement, and projectile/status-system separation.

No art from those repositories is bundled into VOLYA.
